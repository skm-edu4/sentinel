# Data Privacy & GDPR

Last updated: 2026-08-01 | Owner: Legal & Privacy

## Do We Sell Customer Data?

**No.** Sentinel does not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Aggregated, de-identified product analytics may be used to improve the product, but no individual customer record is ever sold. This is a safe, direct answer to give customers — a plain yes or no is acceptable here: **no, we do not sell your data**.

## Third-Party Processors

Data is processed only by subprocessors required to run the service (payment processing, email delivery, cloud hosting). Each is bound by data processing agreements. A current subprocessor list is published at sentinel.example/legal/subprocessors.

## Right to Erasure (GDPR / CCPA Deletion Requests)

- Requests must come from the **verified account email** with the subject line `Privacy: deletion request`.
- Send requests to privacy@sentinel.example.
- **Identity verification is mandatory** before any deletion — support must never delete data on an unverified request, no matter how insistent the customer is.
- Once verified, deletion completes within **30 days**. Backups age out on a 35-day cycle.
- Some records are retained under legal obligation (invoices for the statutory accounting period). Explain this transparently.
- Always confirm in writing when deletion is scheduled; the confirmation itself must come from the privacy team.

## Data Export (Portability)

Customers can self-export their data at Settings → Privacy → Export (JSON or CSV). This is the fastest answer for customers who want a copy rather than deletion.

## Analytics and Cookies

First-party analytics cookies are used; no third-party advertising trackers run on the product. Cookie preferences live under Settings → Privacy → Cookies.
