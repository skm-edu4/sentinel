# Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 2026-09-15 | Owner: Logistics

## Transit Times

- **Standard**: 5 to 7 business days within the continental US. Free on orders over $50.
- **Expedited 2-day**: $12.95, arrives in 2 business days if ordered before 2 PM ET.
- **Overnight**: $24.95, next business day for most US ZIP codes.
- **International (Canada)**: 7 to 10 business days. A customs deposit is collected at checkout and credited on delivery. We do not currently ship outside the US and Canada.

## Tracking Scans

Tracking events update every **24 to 48 hours**. Gaps of 24 to 48 hours between scans are normal, especially at regional hubs.

## Stalled or Lost Packages

- If tracking shows **no movement for 10 or more business days**, the system automatically ships a replacement and files the carrier claim. No customer action is needed; confirm the replacement is on the way.
- If tracking has been static for **fewer than 10 business days**, explain that the package is still within the carrier's investigation window and give the customer the current estimated delivery date. Offer to open a priority trace if the delivery date has already passed.
- For time-critical deliveries (weddings, events, medications), a human agent must handle the escalation regardless of scan age.

## Address Changes

Customers can edit the delivery address themselves within **24 hours of placing the order** via Orders → Edit. After 24 hours or once the label prints, the address can only be changed by the logistics team — escalate.

## Late Deliveries

Standard-policy compensation for a late delivery is **store credit up to $20 per order**. Compensation beyond that amount, or for multiple orders at once, requires human approval.

## Duplicate or Urgent Cases

Never promise delivery dates that are not shown in the tracking system. For urgent cases, quote the fastest available service and note that the customer may switch (price difference applies).
