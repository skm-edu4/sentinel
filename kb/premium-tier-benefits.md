# Premium Tier & Loyalty Benefits

Last updated: 2026-09-05 | Owner: Customer Success

## What Premium Includes

- **Priority support**: first response within 4 business hours (vs. 24 hours on Standard).
- Audit logs (12 months), advanced analytics, and 5 workspaces.
- Phone support callback for accounts with 10 or more seats.

## Enterprise Add-Ons

- **SAML SSO** is available on the **Enterprise plan (10+ seats)** — it is not part of Premium self-serve. Customers requesting SSO must be routed to their Customer Success Manager or to sales for a quote. Never promise a delivery date for SSO features.
- SCIM provisioning and custom data-residency options also ship with Enterprise.

## Loyalty and Renewal Discounts

- Existing customers on annual plans may apply a **10% courtesy discount once per year** at renewal using code **LOYAL10**.
- The code is entered at checkout on the renewal invoice and cannot be combined with other promotions.
- Monthly-plan customers can switch to annual billing at any time to lock the current price before an announced increase.

## Price Increase Communication

When a price increase is announced, existing annual subscribers keep their current price until their next renewal. The new price applies from the renewal date onward. Customers inside the 14-day cooling-off window of a first purchase may cancel for a full refund.

## Discount Requests Outside Policy

Discounts beyond LOYAL10, goodwill credits, or custom pricing require a human agent. If a customer threatens to cancel over pricing, log the feedback tag `pricing-pressure` and escalate to retention.
