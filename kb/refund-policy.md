# Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-01 | Owner: Billing Operations

## Eligibility Window

Refunds may be requested within **30 days of delivery** (or 30 days from the shipment date for digital goods). Requests after the window are reviewed case by case and are rarely approved.

## Refund Methods

- All refunds are issued to the **original payment method only**. We cannot refund to a different card, bank account, or store credit at customer request.
- Once approved, refunds appear on the customer's statement within **5 to 10 business days**, depending on the issuing bank.
- Shipping costs are refunded only when the error is ours (wrong item, damaged item, or our shipping delay).

## Duplicate Charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same order on the same day, this is treated as a duplicate charge. One of the two charges is refunded automatically within **3 business days**. No manager approval is needed for verified duplicate charges.

## Approval Thresholds

- Refunds of **$50 or less**: may be approved automatically by support tooling.
- Refunds **above $50**: always require review and approval by a human agent. Never promise these to a customer without escalation.

## Subscription and Outage Cases

- A renewal charge that posts **after** the customer's cancellation request date is refunded in full automatically within 5 business days.
- Mid-term cancellations of annual plans are not prorated, except within the 14-day cooling-off period for first-time subscribers.
- Service outage credits are handled case by case. Ask the customer for incident timestamps and account ID, then escalate to the billing review queue. Do not promise a specific credit amount.

## How to Respond

Confirm the amount, the payment method, and the expected posting window (5 to 10 business days). Always reference the order number in the reply.
