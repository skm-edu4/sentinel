# Returns & Exchanges

Last updated: 2026-09-01 | Owner: Fulfillment Operations

## Return Window

Items may be returned within **30 days of delivery** in original condition with tags attached. Digital goods and personalized items are not returnable unless defective.

## Wrong Item Received

If the customer received the wrong item:

1. Issue a **prepaid return label** immediately — no approval needed.
2. A replacement ships as soon as the carrier scans the return (does not wait for delivery back to us).
3. If the customer prefers a refund instead of a replacement, follow the refund policy: refunds of $50 or less may be issued directly; above $50 requires human approval.

## Damaged on Arrival

- Request photos of the damage and the packaging.
- With photos, a refund of **up to $50** or a free replacement may be issued immediately.
- Claims above $50 must be escalated to a human agent with the photos attached.

## Refusing Delivery

If a customer refuses delivery at the door or returns to sender, it is treated as a return once the carrier scans it. The refund follows the standard 5 to 10 business day posting window. Customers may refuse delivery inside the 30-day return window without penalty.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order. Size and color exchanges ship with standard delivery at no extra shipping charge when requested within the return window.

## Non-Returnable Situations

Opened software licenses, gift cards, and clearance items marked final sale cannot be returned. Offer troubleshooting help instead when the complaint concerns functionality of a non-returnable item.
