# Billing & Subscription

Last updated: 2026-09-10 | Owner: Billing Operations

## Plans and Billing Dates

- Plans are billed monthly or annually on the original signup date.
- The renewal charge occurs **3 days before** the current term ends; the cancel button remains active until the term end.

## Cancellations

- Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing period**. Access continues until then.
- If a renewal charge posts **after** the cancellation request date, it is refunded in full automatically within 5 business days. Quote this policy confidently to customers charged after cancelling.

## Mid-Cycle Upgrades (Proration)

Upgrading mid-cycle is **prorated automatically**: the customer pays only for the remaining days in the current term at the new plan price, and the new term starts on the next regular billing date. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal — no mid-cycle credit is issued for downgrades.

## Invoices

- Every invoice is available at **Billing → Invoices** for download as PDF.
- Batch download: Billing → Invoices → **Download all** generates a ZIP covering any selected fiscal year.
- Invoices cannot be regenerated with different numbers; corrections are issued as credit notes.

## VAT and Tax

To reissue an invoice with a VAT ID or tax number: the customer replies to the invoice email with the tax ID, or sends it to billing@sentinel.example. Reissued invoices are emailed within **1 business day**.

## Payment Methods

- Expiry/CVV updates for an existing card are self-serve: Settings → Billing → Payment methods.
- If the **card number itself changed** (new card from the bank, or a new corporate card), the account owner must complete identity verification with the billing team before a new card can be attached. Ask for the account email and the last four digits, then escalate to billing if verification is needed.

## PayPal and Alternative Payment Methods

PayPal is **not currently supported**. It is planned for Q1 2027. Corporate wire transfer is available for annual contracts over $1,000.

## Contract Termination and Enterprise Refunds

Early termination, multi-seat contract refunds, and any refund above the $50 auto-approval threshold must go to a human agent — route contract disputes to billing review, never quote a wired-refund timeline.
